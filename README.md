## Introduction
###### API Docs / Introductions

Zeo route planner helps fleet owners create and optimise routes for their drivers. Fleet owners or small businesses who do local last mile deliveries can keep track of delivery status, drivers' live location and send real time updates to the customers.

![](https://zeorouteplanner.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Add-driver.png)

Zeo's mobile apps for delivery drivers are equipped with seamless integrations with all popular navigation tools including waze, tomtom, Google maps, etc. 

Driver has all important details at his finger tips including notes, customer details and parcel count. Zeo handles dynamic address addition and deletion and reroutes the sequence to provide the most optimised route. With Zeo fleet owners save upto 200$ per month per driver and get access to high quality reports like proof of delivery, signatures and delivery time stamps
